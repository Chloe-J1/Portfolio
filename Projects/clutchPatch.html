Clutch Patch

Game descripiton here

Garnier wanted to reach Gen Z/Alpha in Belgium and the Netherlands. For this demographic, normal ways of advertising don't work (AdBlockers, skipping ads, "banner blindness"). The brief wasn't to make a game; it was to find a way for Garnier to exist in the gaming space.

We did this by making a game for Twitch streamers who's goal is to obtain Garnier's pimple patches, so they could remove their pimples before going on stage in front of an audience. Viewers could engage with the game by typing commands in the Twitch livestream chat, this way they could help their favorite streamer to complete the game.
